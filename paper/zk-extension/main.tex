\begin{abstract}
We investigate zero-knowledge extensions of KBFold and the representation of masked binary-field ring switching in Boolean-kernel coordinates. This manuscript develops short, fully quantified mathematical statements with explicit proofs, primary-source citations, and dependencies suitable for Lean~4 certification. Chapter~1 establishes the kernel encoding and normalized fold dictionary. Chapter~2 reconstructs sumcheck, masking, FRI-style folding, and ring switching, including an exact high-degree mask-propagation identity. Chapter~3 defines the finite oracle-proof and zero-knowledge security games, proves adaptive linear-mask distribution lemmas and the Reed--Solomon uniqueness bound, and establishes two obstructions: one-query leakage from unmasked codeword oracles and failure of the unmodified final KBFold closure under high-degree masking. Chapter~4 gives a complete field-independent Boolean-kernel algebra, the exact characteristic-dependent multilinearization/folding relation, degree constraints, and a bounded-query masking theorem for one polynomial oracle. Chapter~5 derives the exact masked fold chain, the revised linear final closure, the enlarged Reed--Solomon degree budget, local fold checks, and a precise joint masking criterion; an explicit attack shows why separately uniform messages may still leak through their correlations. Chapter~6 gives the characteristic-two kernel/Frobenius/trace dictionary, an independent anchored residual basis, and a two-challenge bound for an explicitly timed algebraic coefficient-consistency checker. Chapter~7 establishes lossless binary witness packing, the involutive but non-extension-linear transpose, exact masked incoming/outgoing Ring Switching equations, a conditional algebraic false-claim soundness bound and an explicit disclosure attack when the correction is revealed. Chapter~8 reconstructs the actual Diamond--Posen tensor-algebra ring-switching compiler, proves its embedded-field root bound, derives a conditional $2h/|L|$ sumcheck soundness bound, and demonstrates that the unmasked partial tensor message is not zero knowledge. Chapter~9 proves a perfect causal simulator for the single-opening public-message projection of the masked binary-field interface, analyzes conditional mask preimages, derives a multi-opening pad-reuse leakage attack, and isolates an ideal complete-view simulator and a rigorously conditional external-interface composition theorem. Chapter~10 constructs a concrete classical binary-coordinate affine proof using perfectly hiding Pedersen bit commitments and Schnorr OR proofs, with exact honest-verifier simulation, extraction-based binding reductions, and a rigorously conditional outer soundness theorem. It is neither succinct nor quantum-resistant. Chapter~11 replaces the honest-sigma-verifier restriction with sequential one-bit OR proofs and constructs an abort-corrected classical rewinding simulator. It proves perfect expected-time malicious-verifier zero knowledge for the full one-opening binary-field affine transcript, an exact two-run forking and stand-alone early-extraction bound, and identifies the remaining global temporal extraction and KBFold oracle-composition obligations. Chapter~12 constructs a global classical checkpoint-replay extractor for the sequential one-bit affine compiler. It preserves the main transcript and the fresh outer challenges, extracts one chronologically consistent bit registry or yields a discrete-logarithm violation, and proves the explicit false-claim error bound with a separate external authenticated-oracle term. Chapter~13 specifies a full masked original-field KBFold prover/verifier using authenticated perfectly hiding Pedersen-commitment leaves, committed Sumcheck round coefficients, an exact hidden-offset recurrence and a confidential final degree-one closure. It proves algebraic completeness and the exact online witness-independent distribution of public authenticated shells. The complete proof-transcript simulation and concrete knowledge-extraction security are stated as explicit proof-engine contracts. Chapter~14 proves the final conditional classical one-opening theorem for the fully checked reference protocol: exact completeness, evaluation binding under pre-point NP witness extraction and commitment/hash binding, and a constructive trapdoor-equivocal online ZK simulator under adaptive multi-proof witness indistinguishability. It proves that sparse local folds do not enforce the enlarged RS degree bound and exhibits a quantum false-claim attack against the unchanged Pedersen instantiation. Chapter~15 closes the same manuscript with the final theorem ledger, explicit certification targets and unresolved obligations. No Chapter~16 will be introduced.
\end{abstract}
