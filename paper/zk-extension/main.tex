\begin{abstract}
We investigate zero-knowledge extensions of KBFold and the representation of masked binary-field ring switching in Boolean-kernel coordinates. This manuscript develops short, fully quantified mathematical statements with explicit proofs, primary-source citations, and dependencies suitable for Lean~4 certification. Chapter~1 establishes the kernel encoding and normalized fold dictionary. Chapter~2 reconstructs sumcheck, masking, FRI-style folding, and ring switching, including an exact high-degree mask-propagation identity. Chapter~3 defines the finite oracle-proof and zero-knowledge security games, proves adaptive linear-mask distribution lemmas and the Reed--Solomon uniqueness bound, and establishes two obstructions: one-query leakage from unmasked codeword oracles and failure of the unmodified final KBFold closure under high-degree masking. Chapter~4 gives a complete field-independent Boolean-kernel algebra, the exact characteristic-dependent multilinearization/folding relation, degree constraints, and a bounded-query masking theorem for one polynomial oracle. Chapter~5 derives the exact masked fold chain, the revised linear final closure, the enlarged Reed--Solomon degree budget, local fold checks, and a precise joint masking criterion; an explicit attack shows why separately uniform messages may still leak through their correlations. Chapter~6 gives the characteristic-two kernel/Frobenius/trace dictionary, an independent anchored residual basis, and a two-challenge bound for an explicitly timed algebraic coefficient-consistency checker. Chapter~7 establishes lossless binary witness packing, the involutive but non-extension-linear transpose, exact masked incoming/outgoing Ring Switching equations, a conditional algebraic false-claim soundness bound and an explicit disclosure attack when the correction is revealed. The full zero-knowledge extension of the odd-characteristic KBFold protocol remains a separate theorem to be constructed and proved.
\end{abstract}
