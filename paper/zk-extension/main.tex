\begin{abstract}
We investigate zero-knowledge extensions of KBFold and the representation of masked binary-field ring switching in Boolean-kernel coordinates. The starting point is a precise mathematical evaluation problem, not a new security theorem. This manuscript develops small, fully quantified statements with explicit proofs and dependencies suitable for subsequent Lean~4 certification. Chapter~1 establishes the kernel encoding and its normalized folding dictionary, exhibits witness leakage in an unmasked sumcheck message, and formulates the two field-dependent research tasks.
\end{abstract}
