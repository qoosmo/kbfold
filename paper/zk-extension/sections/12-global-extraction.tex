\section{Global Temporal Extraction and Classical Soundness}\label{sec:global}
The chronological extraction contract left open in
Chapters~10--11 is addressed here \emph{for the explicit
sequential one-bit Schnorr OR affine-proof compiler}.
We retain the same accepted main transcript while
privately replaying each accepted OR statement from
its saved pre-statement classical state.
The resulting \emph{bounded-replay} extractor
assigns one consistent binary-coordinate
registry before each protected outer challenge,
or computes the forbidden Pedersen setup
discrete logarithm.
The construction and its proof rely on
classical checkpoint access, fixed public
statements, a strict epoch schedule and an
independent public-coin verifier.
The underlying authenticating KBFold oracle
remains a separate interface for Chapter~13;
our result does not silently assume its
extractability or quantum soundness.

\subsection{Theorem chain and exact error budget}\label{sec:global:conclusion}
For a sequential OR block with $\kappa$
honest one-bit challenges, let
$p$ be the accepted challenge-string
fraction in a fixed saved classical
prover state. If the main block
accepts, $R$ independent side
attempts fail to find an accepted
\emph{different} challenge string
with joint probability
\[
 \boxed{p(1-p+2^{-\kappa})^R
 \le 2^{-\kappa}+\frac1{R+1}.}
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:global:chronological}
runs this extraction at every accepted
block and restores the original main
post-block state. Its probability of
an accepted main execution with
some missing opening is at most
\[
 \boxed{\varepsilon_{\rm fork}\le
 J\left(2^{-\kappa}+\frac1{R+1}\right).}
\]
The canonical extracted bit values
are fixed before $\theta$ or $z$
according to their epoch, and all
accepted parity checks agree with one
extracted field tuple unless their
conflicting openings compute
$\log_g h$. The fresh outer
challenges retain their independent
uniform distributions.

Consequently the explicit classical
false-claim soundness reduction is
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \varepsilon_{\rm false}
 &\le\min\biggl\{1,\
 \frac{\ell+n}{2^n}
 +\varepsilon_{\rm oracle}
 +\varepsilon_{\rm DL}(R)\\[-1mm]
 &\hspace{30mm}
 +J\left(2^{-\kappa}+\frac1{R+1}\right)
 \biggr\}.
 \end{aligned}}
\]
For any non-negligible false-claim
probability, choosing polynomial
$R$ inversely proportional to that
probability gives a non-negligible
discrete-log solver
(Corollary~\ref{cor:global:advantage}).
Thus, when the field root error,
the external oracle error and
$J2^{-\kappa}$ are negligible,
the \emph{classical affine
component is computationally sound}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:global:asymptotic}).
This is the quantitative
replacement for the formerly
unspecified global temporal
extraction error.

The remaining cryptographic
boundary is \emph{one concrete
authenticated KBFold oracle}
with a proven binding and
joint zero-knowledge simulation
contract. That is the whole
scope of Chapter~13; the
locked manuscript concludes
with Chapter~14 (security)
and Chapter~15 (certification).
