\section{Commitment and Affine-Proof Realization}\label{sec:realize}
The masked Ring Switching interface of Chapter~9 requires
proofs of field-affine relations on a fixed hidden tuple.
This chapter provides an \emph{explicit classical realization
of those relations}: binary-coordinate compilation,
fresh Pedersen commitments, and Schnorr-style OR proofs
for parity constraints. The foundational primitives
are classical~\cite{Pedersen1991,Schnorr1991,CramerDamgardSchoenmakers1994}.
The result is a concrete perfect \emph{honest-sigma-verifier}
zero-knowledge theorem for one opening, with arbitrary causal
choices of the outer Ring Switching challenges.
The exact extraction timing is isolated as a separate
knowledge-soundness contract; full malicious-verifier
zero knowledge, succinctness, quantum security, and
integration with KBFold's original authenticated oracle
are \emph{not} asserted.

\subsection{Main theorem chain and certified scope}\label{sec:realize:conclusion}
For public $a_j,b\in L=\F_{2^n}$ and hidden $x_j\in L$,
Theorem~\ref{thm:realize:affine-to-parity} proves
\[
 \boxed{\sum_ja_jx_j=b
 \iff
 \sum_{j,i}d_k(a_j\gamma_i)d_i(x_j)=d_k(b)
 \quad\forall\,0\le k<n.}
\]
Commit each hidden binary coordinate using the
freshly blinded Pedersen equation
\[
 C_i=g^{b_i}h^{r_i}
\]
in an odd-prime-order group. The commitment is
perfectly hiding and computationally binding
under discrete-log hardness
(Theorems~\ref{thm:realize:binding} and
\ref{thm:realize:parity-binding}).
A parity row is proved by a Schnorr OR proof
that its product commitment opens to an
integer with the correct parity.
Those proofs have perfect completeness,
perfect honest-verifier simulation and
two-transcript special soundness
(Theorems~\ref{thm:realize:parity-complete},
\ref{thm:realize:parity-hvzk},
\ref{thm:realize:or-extract}).

The concrete affine transcript, combined with
the single-opening public core of Chapter~9,
admits an exact simulator for verifiers whose
\emph{inner sigma challenges are honest uniform},
while their \emph{outer} Ring Switching challenges
may be adaptively chosen:
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf{Law}(\mathrm{FullView}_{\rm honest\ sigma})
 =\mathsf{Law}(\mathsf{Sim}_{\rm honest\ sigma}).}
\]
The associated computational soundness implication
requires the early extraction contract:
\[
 \boxed{
 \Pr[\mathrm{false\ evaluation\ accepted}]
 \leq \min\left\{1,
       \frac{\ell+n}{2^n}
       +\varepsilon_{\rm ext}
       +\varepsilon_{\rm DL}\right\}.}
\]
The theorem is \emph{conditional} until the
multi-stage extractor and its exact errors are
constructed and audited.

The raw compiler is not succinct, is not
post-quantum binding, and is not a proof of
malicious-verifier or online multiple-opening
zero knowledge for the original KBFold PCS.
These are subsequent, explicitly identified
research obligations.
