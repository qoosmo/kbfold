\section{Complete Masked KBFold Protocol and Authenticated Oracles}\label{sec:full}
This chapter works in the \emph{original odd-characteristic
field} of KBFold~\cite{MkhidaKBFold2026}.
It assembles the already proved normalized fold
dictionary and degree-aware high-degree masking
(Chapter~5), the genuine KBFold Sumcheck
transcript (Chapter~3), and an authenticated
\emph{hiding} oracle interface.
The public Merkle leaves are fresh Pedersen
commitments to field symbols, rather than
the field symbols themselves. Sumcheck
round coefficients and final folded
values remain hidden behind prescribed
relation proofs; only the authorized
evaluation $v=\widetilde f(z)$ is public.

The protocol defined here uses a
\emph{full-relation} verification mode:
it checks the committed polynomial
words and source-to-fold identities
in a polynomial-size NP circuit.
This is a nonsuccinct reference
protocol with fully specified
algebraic predicates.
A future \emph{sparse} optimization
would retain KBFold's local-query
architecture, but requires a new
adversarial proximity proof for
the enlarged degree budgets.
The relation proof engine is
an explicit cryptographic interface
with circuit-level statements;
its real-world knowledge extraction
and adaptive ZK simulation are the
final security obligations of
Chapter~14. No other research
chapter is introduced.

\subsection{The exact achieved theorem chain}\label{sec:full:conclusion}
For a private table $f$ and mask
$R\in\F[X]_{<B}$, the honest
prover commits to the entire
masked fold chain
\[
 Q_0=U_f^{(m)}+X^{2^m}R,\qquad
 Q_{j+1}=\pfold_{r_j}(Q_j),\qquad
 \deg Q_j<2^{m-j}+\left\lceil B/2^j\right\rceil.
\]
The full masked Sumcheck keeps
its round coefficients and
running offset hidden:
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mu_j(0)+\mu_j(1)&=\sigma_{j-1}+b_{j-1},\\
 \mu_j(r_{j-1})&=\sigma_j+b_j,\\
 \mu_m(r_{m-1})&=\chi_m[X^0]Q_m+b_m.
 \end{aligned}}
\]
The final word has degree at most
one and its hidden constant
coefficient is authenticated
by two distinct final leaves.
Theorem~\ref{thm:full:completeness}
proves perfect algebraic
completeness of the \emph{entire}
declared prover/verifier protocol.

Every public oracle leaf is a
freshly blinded Pedersen
bit-commitment vector.
Theorem~\ref{thm:full:shell-online}
establishes exact \emph{online
witness independence} of the
public commitments, roots,
adaptive authenticated shells
and their paths, even when the
commitment is sent before
the evaluation point is chosen.
The complete view also contains
interactive proof transcripts:
Theorem~\ref{thm:full:conditional-online}
gives their conditional simulation
reduction with an explicit
$\varepsilon_{\rm proofZK}$ contract.

In full-relation mode, accepted
false claims are confined to
the precise pre-knowledge,
relation-soundness, Pedersen
binding and hash-collision events:
\[
 \boxed{
 \varepsilon_{\rm full,snd}
 \le
 \varepsilon_{\rm pre}
 +\varepsilon_{\rm rel}
 +\varepsilon_{\rm bind}
 +\varepsilon_{\rm hash}.}
\]
The direct construction uses
$bM(2-2^{-m})$ public group
elements for fold-word symbols,
and therefore is not a succinct
PCS. The sparse mode must
replace the full circuit
checks using a proved
$\varepsilon_{\rm prox}$ for
the new RS rates. Chapter~14
closes the security theorem
statements in the chosen
classical/ROM/quantum model,
and Chapter~15 is reserved
exclusively for certification
and final manuscript audit.
