\section{Multilinearization, Kernel Folding, and Mask Propagation}\label{sec:kbfoldfold}

This chapter develops the algebraic folding invariants needed for a zero-knowledge extension of KBFold. Its starting point is the Boolean-kernel encoding
\[
 U_f^{(m)}(X)=\sum_{b\in I_m} f(b)K_b^{(m)}(X)
\]
from Chapters~1 and~4, and the normalized odd-characteristic word fold in the original KBFold scheme~\cite{MkhidaKBFold2026}.
We separate four different claims: that the coefficient table is restricted correctly; that polynomial and word folds commute; that an added high-degree mask propagates with a controlled degree budget; and that the \emph{joint} masked oracle answers satisfy a precise linear-algebraic hiding condition. None of these algebraic statements alone asserts a complete zero-knowledge simulator.

\subsection{Main theorem chain and formal scope}\label{sec:kbfoldfold:conclusion}
For $N=2^m$, $1\leq B\leq N$, and $R\in\F[X]_{<B}$, define the high-degree masked encoding
$Q_0=U_f^{(m)}+X^NR$.
The proven chain is
\[
 \boxed{
 Q_j=U_{\mathsf{Res}^{[j]}_{r_{<j}}(f)}^{(m-j)}
     +X^{N/2^j}\mathsf{PFold}^{[j]}_{r_{<j}}(R),}
\]
\[
 \boxed{\deg Q_j<
     \frac{N}{2^j}+\left\lceil\frac{B}{2^j}\right\rceil,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{Q_m(X)=\widetilde f(r)+\rho_r(R)X.}
\]
When $\F$ has odd characteristic and the code domain is a suitable power-of-two multiplicative group, the same polynomial identities propagate to the committed evaluation words and yield degree-correct local fold checks. For fixed challenge vectors and query schedules, the exact hiding condition is the coset-membership criterion of Theorem~\ref{thm:kbff:joint-mask}.

\paragraph{Lean certification boundary.}
Each result above has a self-contained algebraic proof, with all field and indexing hypotheses explicit. The proposed Lean targets are collected in \texttt{CHAPTER\_05\_LEAN\_MAP.md}. A proof that the full KBFold IOP is zero knowledge still requires a masked sumcheck, a simulator for adaptive authenticated openings, and soundness for malicious words in the enlarged Reed--Solomon code; these are not inferred from polynomial identities.
