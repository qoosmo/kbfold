\section{The Diamond--Posen Ring-Switching Compiler}\label{sec:dpr}

We now reconstruct the \emph{original} Diamond--Posen
ring-switching reduction from a small-field
multilinear evaluation to a committed
large-field multilinear evaluation
\cite[Section~4]{DiamondPosen2024}.
Its defining intermediate object is a
tensor algebra $A=L\otimes_K L$
with two distinct field embeddings.
This is not equivalent to treating
binary coordinate transposition as an
extension-field-linear map.
We specialize to $K=\F_2$ and
$[L:K]=2^\ell$ to connect the
construction with Chapters~6--7;
the original compiler applies to
more general finite extensions of
power-of-two degree.

We first prove the ring and column
identities, then the tensor
sumcheck reduction and its
degree-two root lemma, and finally
a conditional composition theorem
and a precise disclosure theorem.
The manuscript distinguishes every
algebraic proof from claims requiring
an authenticated commitment,
a proximity theorem, or
a full zero-knowledge simulator.

\subsection{The theorem chain and its exact scope}\label{sec:dpr:conclusion}
Put $T'(j)=\sum_v\beta_vw_{v,j}$ and
\[
 S=\varphi_1(T')(\varphi_0(r_{\rm hi})),
 \qquad
 H(X)=\varphi_1(T')(X)
      \operatorname{eq}_h(\varphi_0(r_{\rm hi}),X).
\]
The original Boolean evaluation and
tensor sumcheck identities are
\[
 \boxed{
 y_w=C_q(S),\qquad
 \sum_{j\in I_h}H(j)=S,\qquad
 \deg_{X_i}H\leq2.}
\]
A nonzero degree-$d$ polynomial
in $A[X]$ has at most $d$
zeros in the embedded subfield
$\varphi_1(L)$, since distinct
embedded field values differ
by units in $A$.
Consequently, under a fixed
committed packed polynomial and
causal prover timing,
\[
 \boxed{
 \Pr[\text{false original claim accepted}]
 \leq
 \min\{1,2h/q+\varepsilon_{\rm eval}\}.}
\]
This is a conditional algebraic
statement depending on the
soundness of the delegated
large-field evaluation protocol.
The first unmasked tensor message
reveals every $s_v^*$ and is
perfectly distinguishable for
some equal-evaluation witnesses.
Thus the unmasked protocol
does not establish zero knowledge.

Chapter~7's hidden-pad correction
is a \emph{different} verification
interface. It must independently
satisfy soundness, commitment
binding and full adaptive-view
simulation before being claimed
as a zero-knowledge implementation.
