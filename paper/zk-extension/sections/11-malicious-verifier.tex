\section{Malicious-Verifier Zero Knowledge and Temporal Extraction}\label{sec:mv}
Chapter~10 constructed a concrete classical commitment-and-affine
proof with perfect honest-sigma-verifier simulation, but did
not allow the inner sigma challenges to depend on the prover's
first messages. This chapter removes that restriction for the
\emph{concrete binary-coordinate affine transcript}.
The key tool is a sequentially repeated \emph{one-bit}
Schnorr OR proof~\cite{Schnorr1991,CramerDamgardSchoenmakers1994}
and classical verifier rewinding~\cite{Goldreich2001Foundations}.
We derive its exact fixed-challenge law, an abort-corrected
malicious-verifier simulator, a quantitative two-run
forking theorem, and the precise limit of local temporal
knowledge extraction. The underlying KBFold polynomial oracle,
Merkle paths and post-quantum model are \emph{not}
part of this chapter's concrete simulator.

\subsection{The theorem chain and exact achieved security}\label{sec:mv:conclusion}
A single one-bit OR transcript has a perfect
witness-free simulator for each \emph{fixed} verifier
challenge $e\in\{0,1\}$. Its first message is uniform
on $G^d$, independent of the challenge and witness
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:fixed-perfect} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:mv:first-uniform}).
The balanced rewinding simulator accepts one trial
with probability $1/2$, even when the verifier
aborts, and perfectly simulates any classical
schedule-respecting verifier in two expected
trials (Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:one-perfect}).
Sequential composition gives perfect
expected-time \emph{malicious-verifier zero knowledge}
for the full single-opening \emph{affine commitment
and proof transcript}, including adaptive
outer Ring Switching challenges:
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Law}(\mathsf{View}^{P_w,V^*}_{\rm affine})
 =
 \operatorname{Law}(\mathsf{View}^{\mathsf{Sim},V^*}_{\rm affine}).
 }
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:affine-mvzk} does not cover
the original KBFold oracle or its authenticated
openings. A strict polynomial-time simulator
with $M$ mini-proofs and $R$ trials per
mini-proof has statistical error
$\leq M2^{-R}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:truncated}).

For knowledge extraction, two accepted
\emph{distinct} $\kappa$-bit challenge
strings against one reset deterministic
prover expose a common first-message
fork. If its acceptance probability is
$\varepsilon$, the two-run extractor
succeeds with probability at least
\[
 \boxed{
 \max\{0,\varepsilon(\varepsilon-2^{-\kappa})\}.
 }
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:false-parity-bound}
turns an extracted false parity proof
into a discrete-log solver and yields
$\varepsilon\le2^{-\kappa}+
\sqrt{\delta_{\rm DL}}$ for a fixed
statement with known true bit openings.
Its extraction can occur \emph{before}
the protected outer challenge when
the complete OR registration prologue
runs before that challenge
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:standalone-early}).

The stronger \emph{global temporal extraction
coupling} for all interleaved commitments
remains an open formal obligation:
the conditional full false-claim bound is
\[
 \boxed{
 \Pr[\mathrm{false\ claim\ accepted}]
 \leq\min\left\{1,\,
 \frac{\ell+n}{2^n}+
 \varepsilon_{\rm couple}+
 Q\eta_{\rm row}+
 \varepsilon_{\rm oracle}\right\}.
 }
\]
This statement is conditional on the
explicit coupling and authenticated-oracle
hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:mv:conditional-sound}.
The direct bit proof is nonsuccinct,
discrete-log based, and not
post-quantum; the original
odd-characteristic KBFold PCS requires
a separate end-to-end adaptive
zero-knowledge and proximity proof.
