\section{Zero-Knowledge Ring Switching: Public Simulation and Proof Interfaces}\label{sec:zkrs}
This chapter formalizes the masking interface built in Chapters~6--7
and distinguishes three progressively stronger statements:
\emph{perfect privacy of the public field messages},
\emph{perfect complete-view privacy under an opaque ideal proof
functionality}, and \emph{conditional real-world zero knowledge}
requiring a separately proved external-interface simulator.
The order matters. One cannot infer that a cryptographic protocol
is zero knowledge merely because each individual field message
is uniform. The original unmasked tensor compiler is treated
in Chapter~8~\cite{DiamondPosen2024}; the ZK ring-switching
literature is discussed in
\cite{Kochergin2026,FrigoShelat2026}.
We make no claim that our specific binary-field kernel encoding
is a drop-in zero-knowledge extension of the existing
odd-characteristic KBFold PCS.

\subsection{Summary and exact theorem boundary}\label{sec:zkrs:conclusion}

For one admissible opening, sample independent uniform
$p\in L^{I_\ell}$, $p_{\rm in}\in L$, $t\in L^{I_\ell}$
and $\rho\in L$ and define
\[
 m=s^*+p,\quad y'=y+p_{\rm in},\quad c=\mathcal T(p),\quad
 U_k=V_k(p,c,\theta)+t_k^2+t_k,\quad
 A=\rho+\sum_kW_k(z)t_k.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:zkrs:perfect-core} establishes the
\emph{exact} witness-independent joint distribution of
the public core for every causal verifier:
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf{Law}(\mathsf{Core}_{w,V^*})
 =\mathsf{Law}(\mathsf{Sim}_{\rm core}^{V^*}).
 }
\]
All enforced algebraic constraints are affine in hidden
extension-field variables after the public challenges
and messages are fixed, and a nonzero anchored residual
is detected with probability at least $1-n/q$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:zkrs:residual-detection}).
For the combined ideal masked Ring Switching interface,
a false committed evaluation claim is accepted with probability
at most
\[
 \boxed{\varepsilon_{\rm alg}\leq
         \min\{1,(\ell+n)/q\}.}
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:zkrs:ideal-full-zk} gives perfect complete-view
zero knowledge only in a strictly opaque ideal interface.
Theorem~\ref{thm:zkrs:conditional-zk} states the precise
additional requirement on a concrete external proof system:
a sequential witness-independent simulator for its complete
commitment, proof and authenticated-opening transcripts.

Neither result proves real-world zero knowledge for a
fully deployed PCS. The multiple-opening pad-reuse pattern
is insecure (Theorem~\ref{thm:zkrs:reuse-attack}),
and an unmasked original KBFold oracle is not private
(Theorem~\ref{thm:security:unmasked-obstruction}).
The next stage must instantiate and prove the missing
external-interface realization and the odd-characteristic
KBFold-specific adaptive view simulator.
