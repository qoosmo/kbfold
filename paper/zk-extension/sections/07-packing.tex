\section{Binary-Field Packing and Transposition}\label{sec:packing}

This chapter constructs the exact algebraic interface
between a Boolean witness table, its binary field-packed
columns, and a collection of extension-field affine
relations. It builds on the characteristic-two kernel,
trace-dual basis, and anchored consistency checker of
Chapter~6; it rederives the transposition equations of
Diamond--Posen~\cite{DiamondPosen2024} in the chosen
Boolean-kernel coordinates and distinguishes them from
the separate trace-zero masking mechanisms of
Kochergin~\cite{Kochergin2026} and
Frigo--shelat~\cite{FrigoShelat2026}.
Every claim has a declared field and challenge order,
and all algebraic statements are proved in small steps
suitable for later Lean~4 formalization.

\subsection{Main theorem chain}\label{sec:packing:conclusion}

Fix $n=2^\ell$, $L=\F_{2^n}$, and a Boolean
witness $w:I_\ell\times I_h\to\F_2$.
The exact identities established above are
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Unpack}\circ\operatorname{Pack}
        =\operatorname{id},\qquad
 \mathcal T^2=\operatorname{id},}
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal T(s^*)_u
 =\sum_{j\in J}d_u(h_j)\widehat w_j,\qquad
 B_{s^*}(\theta)=\sum_ja_j(\theta)\widehat w_j,}
\]
and, for $m=s^*+p$ with $c=c^p=\mathcal T(p)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 y_{\mathrm{pub}}=\sum_vq_v(m_v+p_v),\qquad
 \sum_ja_j(\theta)\widehat w_j
       =B_m(\theta)+D_c(\theta).}
\]
The first incoming equation is equivalent to
the two concrete affine equations
$\mathsf{CLAIM}$ and $\mathsf{IN}$.
The second is the outgoing affine equation
$\mathsf{OUT}$, and the non-affine identity
$c=\mathcal T(p)$ is checked by the
anchored trace-coordinate checker of Chapter~6.

When a false evaluation $y_{\mathrm{pub}}\neq y_w$
is bound before the independent challenges,
the combined \emph{ideal algebraic} checks obey
\[
 \boxed{
 \Pr[\mathsf{Accept}_7]\leq
 \min\{1,(\ell+n)/2^n\}.}
\]
This is a conditional algebraic result, not a
complete cryptographic theorem.
The chapter also proves that publishing
$(m,c^p)$ reveals $s^*$ exactly, even though
the marginal distribution of $m$ is uniform.
The remaining obligations are external
commitment binding, authenticated affine
proofs, an online joint-view simulator, and
composition with the original odd-characteristic
KBFold PCS.
