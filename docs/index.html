The fold dictionary

Folding a codeword is restricting a table

Over smooth multiplicative domains the classical FRI fold acts on monomial coefficients, so a prover that holds a multilinear polynomial as a table of values must convert it first. In kernel coordinates the conversion disappears: one normalised fold is exactly one sumcheck restriction.

What is proved

Results

Every result specific to the paper is proved in the paper. The only external results used are the correlated-agreement theorem for Reed–Solomon codes and the BCS theorem for interactive oracle reductions.

Theorem 4.8 · Corollary 4.11

Basis theorem

The kernel polynomials form a basis of 𝔽[X]<2n. The change of basis to monomials is a Kronecker power of a 2×2 matrix and costs (n/2)·2n additions.

Theorem 5.3 · Corollary 5.9

Fold dictionary

A normalised FRI fold is the restriction of one variable. Folding the committed codeword ℓ times evaluates the multilinear extension at the challenges.

Theorem 4.13 · Proposition 4.15

Low degree and evaluation

Low degree in kernel coordinates is a sign condition on the table; univariate evaluation is a scaled multilinear evaluation.

Theorem 6.5 · Theorem 7.13

Completeness and soundness

A BaseFold-style commitment in the kernel encoding. Sound and evaluation binding in the unique-decoding regime, with error εfold + 2ℓ/|𝔽| + (1−δ)κ.

Theorem 7.19

Round-by-round knowledge soundness

With an explicit decoding extractor, and a witness bound to the commitment through the fibre distance.

Theorem 7.25 · Corollary 7.26

Post-quantum knowledge soundness

The relaxed round-by-round notion of Chiesa, Di, Hu and Zheng, including partial strings produced by their extractor. Their theorem then gives straightline extraction in the quantum random oracle model, with explicit bounds.

The protocol

Three views of one object

The committed word, the sumcheck table and the folds are the same restricted table at each level. Each round halves the domain, the codeword and the table with one shared challenge.

Roadmap

A foundation for the next protocol

The dictionary is a structural fact about folding, not a single scheme. These are the directions it opens.

Theory

  • List decoding up to the Johnson bound, which needs fewer queries
  • Several openings and batching of one commitment
  • Zero knowledge for the kernel encoding
  • Kernel-type bases for circle and additive domains

Implementation

  • Conformance with the CDHZ compiler (salted Merkle trees, quartic extension)
  • Multiproofs, parallel commit and open, SIMD arithmetic
  • Stable API, documentation, fuzzing

Formal verification

  • A general interactive-oracle-reduction structure in Lean
  • Replace the BCIKS axiom with a proof
  • Link the Rust folding kernel to the Lean specification

Scope. The analysis covers one opening per proof in the unique-decoding regime. The post-quantum corollary applies to the compiler of CDHZ (Construction 11.7); the current Rust code is research code that does not yet implement that compiler. No speed advantage over BaseFold or DeepFold is claimed.
